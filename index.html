Aeon

A private app for one camper van. Last updated October 6, 2026.

What it is

Aeon controls and monitors a single privately owned camper van (lights, climate, power and its security cameras). It is used only by the van's owner and is not offered to anyone else.

Google data

Aeon asks for Google Drive access with the drive.file permission only. It can see just the files it creates itself: video clips from the van's own cameras, kept in a folder named "Aeon Cameras" in the owner's Drive, so the owner can play them back in the app. It cannot see, change or delete any other file in Drive.

No Google data is sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google Drive and the van's own equipment.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The clips stay in Drive until they are deleted there.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on Aeon's Google sign-in screen.
